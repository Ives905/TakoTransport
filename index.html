What We Do

One yard. One crew. One call.

Most activation logistics get split across three or four vendors. Tako Transport handles storage, transport, and on-site labor as a single service, so your equipment has one point of contact from the yard to the event and back.

02 — TRANSPORT

Transport

Point-to-point hauling for stage buses, trailers, and oversized activation builds — local runs and long-haul routes across the region.

03 — INSTALL

Setup & Teardown

On-site installation before the event opens, and full breakdown once it closes — your rig arrives ready and leaves clean.
